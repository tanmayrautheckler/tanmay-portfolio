# Stadium photography

Sources, photographers, and Creative Commons license links are recorded in `credits.json` and displayed in Appearance → Stadium photo credits.

Images are archival stadium views, not representations of current construction or match conditions. Source photographs were resized and converted to WebP. Thumbnails are cropped for cards. The interface applies a colour/saturation overlay. Adapted image files remain available under their source licenses.

The FC Barcelona photograph shows historic Camp Nou; the Real Madrid photograph shows a historic interior of the Santiago Bernabéu. Club theme colours are custom portfolio interpretations, not official brand specifications.

The active Barcelona interior panorama is by Johannes Falke, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Camp_Nou_Panoramic_Interior_View.jpg). The retained earlier `barcelona.webp` and `barcelona-thumb.webp` aerial assets are by Oh-Barcelona.com from Barcelona, Spain, [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/), from [Camp Nou aerial.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Camp_Nou_aerial.jpg); these earlier files are no longer referenced by the interface.
